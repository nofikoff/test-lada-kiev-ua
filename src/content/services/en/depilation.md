Hair removal at Lada N is done two ways — with wax and with sugar paste. The choice between them is not a matter of fashion: paste works better on short, coarse hair and is gentler on sensitive skin, while wax is faster across large areas. Which one suits you is something the therapist will say on the spot, after looking at the area and the hair type.

## Women's hair removal

The list of areas covers the whole body. The most frequent requests are the deep and classic bikini and the underarms; these are areas where accuracy and a pace you are comfortable with matter more than speed. Legs are taken to the knee or in full, arms to the elbow or in full, and the buttocks, stomach and back are listed separately.

The face is a separate item: upper lip, brows and nose need a different approach from large areas and are worked in small sections. If you are coming for the first time, it makes sense to start with one small area — that way you learn your own tolerance before booking a full programme.

## Combinations

Several areas in one visit come out cheaper than the same areas taken separately, and above all they save time. We put together the four combinations ordered most often: bikini with underarms in two depths, and the same with legs to the knee or legs in full added. There is a practical point to it as well: hair in different areas grows back at different rates, and bringing them into one cycle makes the following visits predictable.

## Men's hair removal

Our men's list is wider than the women's and is not a shortened version of it. The back is taken in full or in parts — shoulder blades, shoulders, lower back; the stomach works the same way, in full or as a strip. The chest, the intergluteal strip and the buttocks in full are listed separately.

Arms and legs are taken to the bend or in full, and there is the neck along with small facial areas — ears, nose, brows, cheeks and areolas. Men's hair is coarser and sits deeper, so the first session is almost always felt more than the ones that follow; after that the hair grows back finer and the procedure gets easier.

## What to know before the visit

The hair needs a few millimetres of growth — paste and wax simply cannot grip hair that is too short, and the session would have to be moved. Regularity matters more here than any single visit: when areas are treated on a schedule, the hair comes back finer and sparser, and the procedure becomes easier every time.

Do not plan hair removal for a day when you are going straight to the beach, a pool or a sauna afterwards: the skin needs time. On the day of the procedure it is better to skip the scrub.
